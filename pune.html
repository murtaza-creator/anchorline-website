Mumbai to Pune is not a supply chain problem

Roughly three hours by road. Practically the same project zone.

Pune is close enough to Mumbai that Anchorline executes Pune-area hospitality and commercial projects directly from our Mumbai base — with the same attention to dye-lot consistency, sample approvals, and delivery coordination that we give every Mumbai project. Supply Chain Reality

Why a well-coordinated Mumbai supplier is often faster than local

The assumption that a local Pune vendor is the reliable choice is worth examining. What determines project reliability is supply chain depth, material access and coordination capability — not proximity to the site.

Same dye-lot consistency, regardless of delivery city

Dye-lot matching across a multi-room project is a production and QC discipline, not a geography one. Our QC process at the mill and supplier level is the same whether the project is in Andheri or Aundh.

Physical sample approval before any production commitment

We send physical samples to the project team before production begins — always. This is the site's stated policy and it applies equally to Pune projects. No sign-off on a screen render; you approve the actual material first.

Pan-India delivery infrastructure, already in place

Anchorline already delivers across India. Pune is not a special case or a stretch — it is a standard delivery destination. Transit time from our Mumbai base to a Pune site is typically one to two days for road freight.

One point of contact for the full brief

A single contact manages your project from initial enquiry through sample approval, production, QC and delivery. This is not a feature unique to Pune projects — it is how Anchorline works for every client, in every city.
