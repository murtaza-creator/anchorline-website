What We Supply

Flooring solutions built for hospitality scale

FR-Certified Carpet Tiles
Fire-rated modular tiles for corridors, conference rooms and back-of-house areas. Replaceable panels reduce long-term maintenance cost.
Axminster Carpet
Woven to pattern, room by room. Precise dye-lot matching across every guest room, corridor and lobby in your property.
Nylon Printed Carpet
High-traffic broadloom for banquet halls and convention spaces. Printed nylon holds colour and pattern under heavy use.
Broadloom Carpet
Seamless wall-to-wall installation for suites and executive floors. Available in cut pile, loop pile and cut-loop constructions.
Custom Lobby Rugs
Hand-tufted rugs designed to your brand palette and lobby dimensions. Statement pieces that photograph well and last for years.
Corridor Runners
Custom-width runners with consistent pile weight from floor one to floor twenty. Dye-lot guaranteed across the full project order.
How It Works

From hotel brief to installation-ready carpet

Step 01
Share your hotel brief
