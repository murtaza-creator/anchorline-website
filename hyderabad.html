Pan-India Delivery

What "pan-India supply" actually means in practice

Anchorline states pan-India delivery capability on its main site. This section is specific about what that means for a Hyderabad project, so there are no assumptions.

Physical sample to your Hyderabad address before production

The sample step is not waived for pan-India projects. A tufted swatch or physical sample is dispatched to the project address — in Hyderabad — before any full production commitment is made. You approve the actual material, not a photograph.

Dye-lot consistency is a production process, not a proximity one

Matching dye lots across a multi-room or multi-floor project is managed at the mill and QC stage — before any material leaves production. The delivery city does not affect this. What matters is that the specification is fixed and the QC sign-off happens before dispatch.

Freight coordination and documentation, included

Pan-India delivery includes freight coordination and all necessary documentation. Hyderabad is a standard freight destination from Mumbai. Transit time by road freight is typically two to three days; by rail or air freight, faster when the brief requires it.

Single point of contact from brief to delivery

One contact manages your Hyderabad project end to end — initial enquiry, material selection, sample dispatch and approval, production, QC, and delivery coordination. There is no handoff to a regional team or third-party agent.
