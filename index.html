Hand-Tufted
Hand-Tufted Rugs

Yarn punched through a backing cloth with a tufting gun, hand-sheared and finished. Faster to produce than hand-knotted, with excellent pattern fidelity. The right choice for large-format lobby rugs, custom shapes, and logo or medallion pieces where timeline matters.

  • Custom shapes & logo / medallion pieces
  • Large-format lobby and corridor rugs
  • Shorter lead time: [X–Y weeks, confirm with supply]
  • Wool, wool-nylon blend, or viscose pile
Hand-Knotted
Hand-Knotted Rugs

Each knot tied individually on a loom, the construction behind fine carpets and heirloom rugs. Denser, more durable, finer pattern resolution. For spaces meant to last decades: boardrooms, VIP suites, signature dining rooms where the rug is the design.

  • Finest pattern detail and texture
  • Boardrooms, suites, and signature spaces
  • Longer lead time: [X–Y weeks, confirm with supply]
  • New Zealand wool, Tibetan wool, or silk highlights

How a Custom Rug Gets Made

From brief to finished piece

01
Brief & Inspiration
Share a sketch, mood board, brand motif, or an existing design to adapt. We confirm size, shape, and colourway direction.
02
Design & CAD
Rendered to scale with colourway options for sign-off before production begins. CAD gets the proportions right, but it does not replace a physical sample.
03
Physical Sample
Required, not optional. A tufted or woven swatch is produced for your approval. True colour and texture can only be confirmed in hand, not on screen.
04
Weaving & QC
Once the sample is approved, the rug is made, trimmed, and quality-checked against that approved sample: pile, colourway, and finish all verified.
05
Delivered to Site
Sized and finished for the space it was designed for. Delivered on schedule with full documentation. Pan-India delivery.
